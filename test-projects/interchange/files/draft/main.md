# Ketamine for treatment-resistant depression: a bendable-dose trial

## Background

A single sub-anaesthetic infusion of ketamine produced rapid antidepressant effects
in the first placebo-controlled trial [@Berman2000], and the effect was replicated in
treatment-resistant depression six years later [@Zarate2006; @Berman2000]. Response
at 24 hours has since been reported in most controlled series [@Zarate2006, p. 858].
