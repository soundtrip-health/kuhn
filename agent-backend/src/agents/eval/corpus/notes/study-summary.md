# MIRAS-T2D — study summary (results as reported)

Phase II randomized, double-blind, placebo-controlled trial. 2,482 patients
with type 2 diabetes and established cardiovascular disease were randomized
1:1 to mirasatide (n=1,240) or placebo (n=1,242). Median follow-up was 2.9
years.

## Primary endpoint
The composite of cardiovascular death, nonfatal myocardial infarction, and
nonfatal stroke (MACE) occurred in 98 of 1,240 patients (7.9%) on mirasatide
versus 120 of 1,242 (9.7%) on placebo: hazard ratio 0.82 (95% CI 0.71–0.94),
p=0.008.

## Key secondary endpoints
- Hospitalization for heart failure: 61 vs 83 events; HR 0.74 (95% CI
  0.61–0.90), p=0.003.
- All-cause mortality: 34 vs 41 deaths; HR 0.79 (95% CI 0.57–1.10), p=0.16
  (not statistically significant).
- Change in HbA1c at week 52: −1.4 percentage points (95% CI −1.6 to −1.2)
  with mirasatide versus −0.2 with placebo.

## Safety
Nausea was more frequent with mirasatide (23% vs 14%, p=0.002). Rates of
diabetic ketoacidosis and pancreatitis were similar between groups. No
between-group difference in serious adverse events overall.
