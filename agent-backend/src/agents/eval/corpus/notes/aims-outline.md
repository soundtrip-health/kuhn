# MIRAS-T2D R01 — Specific Aims outline (source notes)

Working title: Sustaining Cardiovascular Benefit After Myocardial Infarction with
Mirasatide (POST-MIRAS R01)

## Context
MIRAS-T2D (phase II, n=2,482) showed that mirasatide, a twice-daily GLP-1 receptor
agonist, reduced the composite of cardiovascular death, nonfatal myocardial
infarction, and nonfatal stroke (MACE) by 18% versus placebo in type 2 diabetes
patients with established cardiovascular disease (HR 0.82, 95% CI 0.71–0.94,
p=0.008). Hospitalization for heart failure fell 26% (HR 0.74, 95% CI 0.61–0.90,
p=0.003). All-cause mortality was not significantly different
(HR 0.79, 95% CI 0.57–1.10, p=0.16).

Gap: MIRAS-T2D enrolled stable outpatients 6+ months after a recent event. We do
not know whether starting mirasatide in the vulnerable window after an acute
myocardial infarction — when secondary prevention adherence is lowest and
biomarker trajectories shift fastest — preserves the benefit, or whether the
biomarker response (change in NT-proBNP and CRP at 12 weeks) predicts which
patients will have MACE over 3 years.

## Aim 1 — Efficacy in the post-MI window (primary)
Enroll 1,500 patients 7–90 days post-MI (STEMI or NSTEMI, with or without
revascularization) who also have type 2 diabetes. Randomize 1:1 to mirasatide
versus placebo, open-label, on top of guideline-directed therapy. Primary
endpoint: MACE at 3 years. Interim analysis at 1,050 events with a DSMB.

## Aim 2 — Biomarker mechanisms and prediction
Serial NT-proBNP, CRP, and HbA1c at baseline, 12 weeks, 6 months, and 12 months
in a nested cohort (n=600). Objectives: (a) characterize the early biomarker
response, (b) build and validate a 12-week risk model for 3-year MACE,
(c) test whether the biomarker response mediates the treatment effect.

## Aim 3 — Long-term safety and adherence
3-year follow-up of the full cohort: all-cause mortality, heart-failure
hospitalization, treatment discontinuation, and incident diabetes
complications. Substudy: a patient-reported outcomes instrument at 3, 12, and
36 months to characterize adherence decay in the post-MI population.

## Resources and team
PI (applicant) — cardiovascular outcomes research; co-PI — biostatistics.
Existing assets: MIRAS-T2D trial data and biobank, a standing CRN of 14 US
sites, and an NIH R01 in biomarker prediction (awarded 2025) with overlapping
personnel.

## Timeline
Year 1: finalize protocol, IRB, site activation (8 of 14 sites by month 10).
Year 2: enrollment complete (n=1,500 by month 24); nested-cohort biobanking.
Year 3: 3-year follow-up complete for the first third of the cohort; interim
analysis.
Year 4: primary analysis; biomarker model validation.
Year 5: confirmatory analyses, final report, manuscript submission.
