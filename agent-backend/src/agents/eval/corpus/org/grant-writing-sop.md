# Grant Writing SOP (org knowledge)

This standard operating procedure applies to all NIH grant applications
prepared under this organization. It reflects the organization's current
policies as of the 2025 planning cycle.

## Specific Aims page
- Maximum length: 1 page.
- The page MUST state the expected duration of the project (start and end
  year, or number of years).
- Each aim must be a single, testable hypothesis with its primary endpoint
  named explicitly.
- No more than three aims.

## Research plan
- Maximum 12 pages total for the entire research plan (excluding references).
- References do not count toward the page limit but must follow the NIH style
  (no more than 50 references).

## Budget
- Total direct costs must not exceed $4,500,000 over the full project period.
- F&A (indirect) costs are charged at the negotiated institutional rate; no
  F&A is charged on subcontractor direct costs.
- Personnel effort must not exceed 50.0 FTE in any single year.
- The budget justification is a separate document with no page limit, but the
  budget tables themselves must fit within 12 pages.

## Key personnel
- A full NIH biosketch is required for the PI and for at least one co-investigator.
- Every person listed as a key extramural scientist or a recipient of
  project funds must have a biosketch.
- The CVs of trainees (if any) must be included in the supplementary materials.

## Submission checklist
- Confirm the budget is within the $4,500,000 total-direct-cost cap before
  submission.
- Confirm the Specific Aims page states the project duration.
- Confirm all key personnel have biosketches on file with the grants office.
