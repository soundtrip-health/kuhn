# Biosciences Research Standards Knowledge Library

Authoritative, freely accessible reporting standards, nomenclature guidelines, and data-sharing frameworks for life sciences research. Organized by domain with canonical URLs, access information, and brief descriptions.

---

## Animal Research Reporting

### ARRIVE 2.0: Guidelines for Reporting Animal Research

- **Publisher/Authority:** ARRIVE Guidelines Collaborative (endorsed by PLOS Biology)
- **Canonical URL:** https://www.arriveguidelines.org/
- **Format:** PDF checklist, web-based guidance, elaboration documents
- **Access/License:** Open access (published in PLOS Biology, July 2020)
- **Description:** Updated guidelines for in vivo animal research reporting with an "Essential 10" minimum checklist and comprehensive guidance. Widely adopted by journals and funding agencies to improve transparency and reproducibility of preclinical research.

## Omics Data Standards

### MIAME: Minimum Information About a Microarray Experiment

- **Publisher/Authority:** MGED Society (Gene Expression Omnibus, NCBI)
- **Canonical URL:** https://www.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/geo/info/MIAME.html
- **Format:** Web guidelines, submission requirements
- **Access/License:** Open access via NCBI
- **Description:** Defines six essential information categories (raw data, processed data, sample annotations, experimental design, feature annotations, protocols) required for microarray experiment reporting. Mandatory for major journals and GEO/ArrayExpress submission.

### MINSEQE: Minimum Information About Sequencing Experiments

- **Publisher/Authority:** FGED Society (Functional Genomics Data Society)
- **Canonical URL:** https://www.fged.org/projects/minseqe/
- **Format:** PDF specification, Zenodo archive (https://zenodo.org/record/5706412)
- **Access/License:** Open access, archived after FGED closure
- **Description:** Parallel standard to MIAME for next-generation sequencing, requiring biological system documentation, FASTQ sequence data, processed summary data, experiment overviews, and detailed protocols for data interpretation and reproducibility.

### MIQE 2.0: Real-Time Quantitative PCR Reporting Guidelines

- **Publisher/Authority:** MIQE Collaboration, published in Clinical Chemistry (Oxford Academic, 2025)
- **Canonical URL:** https://academic.oup.com/clinchem/article/71/6/634/8119148
- **Format:** PDF guidelines, 85-item checklist (expanded in 2025 revision)
- **Access/License:** Open access version available; adopted by major journals
- **Description:** Standardizes reporting of qPCR experiments covering sample handling, assay design, validation, and quantification cycle (Cq) reporting with efficiency-corrected quantification and prediction intervals. Recent 2025 update reflects advances in qPCR technology and methodology.

## Data Stewardship & FAIR Principles

### FAIR Guiding Principles for Scientific Data Management

- **Publisher/Authority:** Scientific Data consortium (Wilkinson et al., 2016)
- **Canonical URL:** https://doi.org/10.1038/sdata.2016.18 (original publication in Scientific Data 3:160018)
- **Format:** Published article + implementation guides
- **Access/License:** Open access
- **Description:** Foundational framework defining Findability, Accessibility, Interoperability, and Reusability of scientific data. Increasingly adopted by funding agencies and journals; emphasizes machine-readable metadata and persistent identifiers over open-access alone.

## Gene & Protein Nomenclature

### HGNC: HUGO Gene Nomenclature Committee

- **Publisher/Authority:** HUGO International (US NHGRI & Wellcome Trust funding)
- **Canonical URL:** https://www.genenames.org/
- **Format:** Web database, downloadable nomenclature files, guidelines documents
- **Access/License:** Open access; non-profit resource
- **Description:** Official international authority for human gene naming, maintaining 43,000+ approved symbols (19,200 protein-coding, 9,000 ncRNA, 14,000 pseudogenes) with standardized nomenclature across species and clear guidelines for novel gene naming.

### International Protein Nomenclature Guidelines

- **Publisher/Authority:** UniProt Consortium (EMBL-EBI, NCBI, PIR, Swiss Institute of Bioinformatics)
- **Canonical URL:** https://www.uniprot.org/help/international_protein_nomenclature_guidelines
- **Format:** Web guidelines, annotation standards documentation
- **Access/License:** Open access via UniProt
- **Description:** Coordinated guidelines for consistent protein naming emphasizing uniqueness, unambiguity, ortholog compatibility, and alignment with official gene nomenclature. Essential for literature retrieval and computational integration across protein databases.

### NCBI Taxonomy Database

- **Publisher/Authority:** National Center for Biotechnology Information (NIH)
- **Canonical URL:** https://www.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/Taxonomy/
- **Format:** Interactive database, downloadable classification files, curated nomenclature
- **Access/License:** Open access
- **Description:** Manually curated standard nomenclature and phylogenetic classification for all organisms in biological sequence databases (GenBank, ENA, DDBJ). Follows formal nomenclature codes (ICBN, ICZN, ICTV) with seven universal ranks plus specialized sub-ranks.

## Genomic Data Sharing & Standards

### GA4GH: Global Alliance for Genomics and Health

- **Publisher/Authority:** Global Alliance for Genomics and Health (500+ member organizations)
- **Canonical URL:** https://www.ga4gh.org/
- **Format:** Technical standards, policy frameworks, open-source tools
- **Access/License:** Open source implementations, freely available specifications
- **Description:** Develops interoperable technical standards and policy frameworks enabling responsible international genomic data sharing across 3 million+ genomes. Coordinates with FHIR, OMOP, Human Phenotype Ontology and other key terminologies.

### NCBI Submission Guidelines (GenBank, dbVar, dbGaP)

- **Publisher/Authority:** National Center for Biotechnology Information (NIH)
- **Canonical URL:** https://www.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/genbank/submit/
- **Format:** Web submission guides, portal documentation, format specifications
- **Access/License:** Open access; supports open science via NCBI public databases
- **Description:** Comprehensive submission requirements for sequence data (GenBank), structural variants (dbVar), and phenotype-genotype studies (dbGaP) with specific guidance for prokaryotic/eukaryotic genomes, whole-genome assemblies, and variant types. Note: effective June 16, 2026, personal human genomic data from private individuals no longer accepted.

## Research Rigor & Reproducibility

### NIH Guidance: Rigor and Reproducibility in Grant Applications

- **Publisher/Authority:** National Institutes of Health (NOT-OD-16-011, updated 2020)
- **Canonical URL:** https://grants.nih.gov/policy-and-compliance/policy-topics/reproducibility/guidance
- **Format:** Policy guidance, FAQ, infographics, institutional training resources
- **Access/License:** Open access (public policy)
- **Description:** Mandated guidance (effective January 2016+) requiring NIH applicants to address four core areas: rigor of prior research, rigorous experimental design, consideration of biological variables (sex), and authentication of key resources. Includes specific requirements for fellowship/training awards.

### STROBE: Strengthening the Reporting of Observational Studies in Epidemiology

- **Publisher/Authority:** STROBE Initiative (international multidisciplinary collaboration)
- **Canonical URL:** https://www.strobe-statement.org/
- **Format:** 22-item checklist, explanation/elaboration document, conference abstract extension
- **Access/License:** Open access; adopted by 100+ major journals
- **Description:** Evidence-based 22-item checklist for reporting cohort, case-control, and cross-sectional observational studies. Addresses variable quality in study reporting; guidelines clarify reporting expectations without prescribing research design choices.

## Image & Microscopy Standards

### Guidelines for Publishing Images in Life Science Research

- **Publisher/Authority:** QUAREP-LiMi (Quality Assessment and Reproducibility for Instruments & Images in Light Microscopy, 554 members, 39 countries)
- **Canonical URL:** https://www.nature.com/collections/djiciihhjh (Nature microscopy collection)
- **Format:** Checklists, published standards in Nature Methods
- **Access/License:** Open access
- **Description:** Community-developed guidelines addressing microscopy image acquisition, processing, and analysis reporting. Provides checklists for image focusing, cropping, resizing, and critical metadata (laser power, exposure time, objective specifications) to improve reproducibility in bioimaging research.

## Structural Biology Data

### Protein Data Bank (PDB) & PDBx/mmCIF Standard

- **Publisher/Authority:** Worldwide Protein Data Bank (wwPDB) consortium (international governance since 2003)
- **Canonical URL:** https://www.rcsb.org/ (RCSB Protein Data Bank)
- **Format:** 3D structure database, mmCIF/PDBx data standard, validation tools
- **Access/License:** Open access; FAIR principles compliance
- **Description:** Global repository for 3D macromolecular structures (established 1971). PDBx/mmCIF is the standardized format for crystallography, NMR, and cryo-EM structure data. wwPDB partners collaboratively oversee deposition, validation, biocuration, and versioning of 200,000+ structure entries.

## Mass Spectrometry & Proteomics

### SMART: Data Reporting Standard for Mass Spectrometry Imaging

- **Publisher/Authority:** Journal of Mass Spectrometry, Xi et al. (2023)
- **Canonical URL:** https://analyticalsciencejournals.onlinelibrary.wiley.com/doi/10.1002/jms.4904
- **Format:** Published standard, implementation guidance
- **Access/License:** Open access version available
- **Description:** Unified reporting system capturing essential baseline information for mass spectrometry imaging (MSI) experiments including sample preparation, data acquisition, instrument parameters, data processing, and analysis methods for cross-laboratory comparability.

### MIAMSIE/MSIcheck: Mass Spectrometry Imaging Reporting

- **Publisher/Authority:** Bioanalytical research community (GigaScience, 2018)
- **Canonical URL:** https://www.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/pmc/articles/PMC6203951/
- **Format:** Reporting supplement checklist
- **Access/License:** Open access
- **Description:** Minimum Information About a Mass Spectrometry Imaging Experiment (MIAMSIE) and abbreviated MSIcheck version establish sufficient detail requirements across sample preparation, data acquisition, data characteristics, processing, and analysis to support reproducibility.

## Metabolomics Data Standards

### COSMOS: Coordination of Standards in Metabolomics

- **Publisher/Authority:** Metabolomics Standards Initiative (EU FP7 Initiative, launched 2012)
- **Canonical URL:** https://www.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/pmc/articles/PMC4605977/
- **Format:** Data infrastructure, metadata standards, ISA-tab format implementation
- **Access/License:** Open access; coordinated via MetaboLights repository
- **Description:** Develops robust data infrastructure and exchange standards for metabolomics metadata and data annotation. Implements MSI standards for minimum metadata reporting; supports data sharing, integration, and re-utilization across laboratories through standardized formats and repositories.

## Single-Cell & High-Throughput Analysis

### Practical Handbook on Single-Cell RNA Sequencing Quality Control

- **Publisher/Authority:** Frontiers in Cell and Developmental Biology (peer-reviewed)
- **Canonical URL:** https://www.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/pmc/articles/PMC12403637/
- **Format:** Review guide, quality control checklist, analysis best practices
- **Access/License:** Open access
- **Description:** Comprehensive guide addressing variable standards in scRNA-seq design and analysis. Emphasizes quality control and preprocessing standardization; recommends ≥500 cells per cell type per individual for reliable quantification, though acknowledges ongoing challenges in establishing universal gold standards.

## Cell Culture & Resource Authentication

### ICLAC: International Cell Line Authentication Committee

- **Publisher/Authority:** ICLAC (formed 2012, coordinated via Labcorp)
- **Canonical URL:** https://iclac.org/
- **Format:** Authentication guides, STR profile databases, misidentified cell line register
- **Access/License:** Open access
- **Description:** International authority promoting cell line authentication through STR profiling (ANSI/ATCC ASN-0002-2022 standard). Maintains curated register of misidentified/contaminated cell lines; provides guides for human and mouse authentication and institutional best practices checklist.

### Guide to Human Cell Line Authentication

- **Publisher/Authority:** ICLAC
- **Canonical URL:** https://iclac.org/wp-content/uploads/ICLAC_Guide-to-Human-Cell-Line-Authentication_02-Mar-2023.pdf
- **Format:** PDF guide (2023 revision)
- **Access/License:** Open access
- **Description:** Step-by-step guidance for human cell line authentication emphasizing STR profiling as the gold standard. Provides practical implementation details and institutional recommendations for integrating authentication into routine culture practice and research reporting.

## Biosafety & Research Governance

### Laboratory Biosafety Guidance

- **Publisher/Authority:** World Health Organization (WHO)
- **Canonical URL:** https://www.who.int/publications/i/item/9789240095113
- **Format:** Guidance document, policy framework
- **Access/License:** Open access
- **Description:** WHO guidance for laboratory biosafety including institutional oversight, risk assessment, and containment level selection. Applicable to dual-use research of concern (DURC) and enhanced pathogen research requiring pre-publication biosecurity review and access-controlled data management.

### Biosecurity in an Age of Open Science

- **Publisher/Authority:** National Academies Press (consensus study)
- **Canonical URL:** https://www.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/pmc/articles/PMC9009689/
- **Format:** Published analysis, policy discussion
- **Access/License:** Open access
- **Description:** Examines tension between open science principles and biosecurity needs in research with misuse potential. Recommends access-controlled repositories and APIs for sensitive research (viral engineering, pathogen enhancement) to preserve open science benefits while mitigating dual-use risks.

## Data Availability & Publication Standards

### PLOS Data Availability Policy

- **Publisher/Authority:** PLOS (Public Library of Science)
- **Canonical URL:** https://journals.plos.org/plosbiology/s/data-availability
- **Format:** Journal policy, author guidelines
- **Access/License:** Open access policy
- **Description:** Mandates Data Availability Statements specifying where minimum datasets reside with persistent identifiers (DOIs, accession numbers). Requires transparent access conditions and repository selection appropriate to data type (e.g., ArrayExpress for microarray, GenBank for sequences).

### Nature Reporting Standards

- **Publisher/Authority:** Nature Portfolio (Springer Nature)
- **Canonical URL:** https://www.nature.com/nature-portfolio/editorial-policies/reporting-standards
- **Format:** Editorial policy, checklists, data repository guidance
- **Access/License:** Open access policy
- **Description:** Comprehensive reporting standards covering experimental design, statistics, code/protocol availability, and data availability statements. Covers life sciences, physical sciences, and engineering with field-specific checklists ensuring transparency and reproducibility in published research.
