# Regulatory Guidance Knowledge Library

Authoritative, freely accessible regulatory guidance documents for scientific writing in biosciences: drug development, biologics, medical devices, and software-as-medical-device. All FDA documents are US government public domain works. ICH guidelines are publicly available for download. EMA scientific guidelines are freely accessible online.

---

## FDA Drug & Biologics Development (CDER/CBER)

### Common Technical Document & Submission Format

**Electronic Common Technical Document (eCTD)**  
Authority: FDA, Center for Drug Evaluation and Research  
URL: https://www.fda.gov/drugs/electronic-regulatory-submission-and-review/electronic-common-technical-document-ectd  
Format: Guidance + technical specifications  
Access: Public domain (US government work)  
Overview: FDA portal and guidance for mandatory eCTD format (v4.0 supported since 2024) for NDAs, ANDAs, BLAs, and INDs. Includes submission requirements, technical specifications, and organization hierarchy.

**M4: The CTD — Organization (General Considerations)**  
Authority: FDA  
URL: https://www.fda.gov/regulatory-information/search-fda-guidance-documents/m4-organization-common-technical-document-registration-pharmaceuticals-human-use-guidance-industry  
Format: PDF guidance document  
Access: Public domain  
Overview: Comprehensive guidance on organizing NDA/BLA content according to ICH Common Technical Document (CTD) structure for all submission types. Covers Module 2–5 organization, cross-referencing, and quality.

**M4Q: The CTD — Quality**  
Authority: FDA  
URL: https://www.fda.gov/media/71581/download  
Format: PDF guidance document  
Access: Public domain  
Overview: Quality module (Module 3) content requirements including analytical methods, drug substance/product specifications, shelf-life/storage data, and quality overall summaries for drug and biologics applications.

**M4S: The CTD — Safety**  
Authority: FDA  
URL: https://www.fda.gov/media/185340/download  
Format: PDF guidance document  
Access: Public domain  
Overview: Nonclinical study report organization and content requirements for Module 4, covering pharmacology, toxicology, ADME, and other preclinical safety data presentation in CTD format.

**M4E(R2): The CTD — Efficacy**  
Authority: FDA  
URL: https://www.fda.gov/media/93569/download  
Format: PDF guidance document  
Access: Public domain  
Overview: Clinical efficacy module (Module 5) structure and content requirements including clinical study reports, clinical pharmacology summaries, and clinical overview documents for NDA/BLA submissions.

**M2 eCTD: Electronic Common Technical Document**  
Authority: FDA  
URL: https://www.fda.gov/media/71513/download  
Format: PDF technical guidance  
Access: Public domain  
Overview: Technical implementation guidance for eCTD electronic submission format, including file structure, naming conventions, and conformance requirements for FDA submissions.

**eCTD Submission Requirements: What You Need to Know**  
Authority: FDA  
URL: https://www.fda.gov/files/drugs/published/eCTD-Submission-Requirements--What-You-Need-to-Know.PDF

Overview: Practical checklist and summary of mandatory eCTD requirements, technical specifications (v4.0), sequence numbering, and submission procedures for drugs and biologics.

## Notes for Users

- **FDA Documents**: All FDA guidance documents are US government public domain works and may be freely used, reproduced, and distributed.
- **ICH Guidelines**: Available freely on the ICH database; adopted by FDA, EMA, and PMDA. No registration required.
- **EMA Guidelines**: Published on ema.europa.eu; adopted scientific guidelines form the official regulatory framework for EU submissions.
- **URL Stability**: All URLs verified as of August 2026. FDA and EMA URLs are canonical institutional addresses. ICH database URLs link to PDF guideline documents directly.
- **Updates**: Regulatory guidance evolves; check issuing authority websites for latest revisions and Q&As (e.g., ICH E6(R3) finalized January 2025).
