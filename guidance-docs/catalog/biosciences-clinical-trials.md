# Clinical Trials Knowledge Library

A curated collection of authoritative, freely accessible guidance documents for clinical trial design, conduct, reporting, and ethics in the biosciences.

## EQUATOR Network Directory

The EQUATOR Network maintains a comprehensive, freely accessible database of **700+ reporting guidelines** across health research study designs, specialties, and interventions. Major categories include:

- **CONSORT family** (RCTs + extensions for cluster, crossover, multi-arm, AI, equity, pediatric designs)
- **SPIRIT family** (trial protocols + design variants)
- **PRISMA family** (systematic reviews, scoping reviews, IPD syntheses, living reviews)
- **STROBE family** (cohort, case-control, cross-sectional studies + variants)
- **Domain-specific** (STARD diagnostics, CARE case reports, TRIPOD prediction models, GRADE evidence quality)
- **Emerging methods** (AI, machine learning, digital health, telehealth)
- **Discipline-specific** (oncology, surgery, rehabilitation, dentistry, psychiatry)

All guidelines are freely available as checklists, full-text publications in open-access journals, and accompanied by explanation & elaboration documents.

**EQUATOR Network Directory**: https://www.equator-network.org/reporting-guidelines/

## How to Use This Library

1. **Select reporting guideline** based on study design: CONSORT (RCTs), STROBE (observational), PRISMA (reviews), STARD (diagnostics), CARE (case reports).
2. **Reference regulatory/ethical guidance** (ICH E6/E8/E9, Declaration of Helsinki) during protocol development and site training.
3. **Integrate intervention description** (TIDieR) and patient involvement (SPIRIT/CONSORT 2025) into study design.
4. **Register trial early** on ClinicalTrials.gov before enrollment.
5. **Prepare informed consent** using FDA guidance (plain language, 8th-grade level, teach-back verification).
6. **Implement data integrity systems** per ICH guidelines; establish safety monitoring per protocol.
7. **Report results** within 12 months of primary completion (ClinicalTrials.gov); prepare lay summary per EU/ISMPP guidance.
8. **Publish** with completed CONSORT checklist for peer-reviewed submission.
