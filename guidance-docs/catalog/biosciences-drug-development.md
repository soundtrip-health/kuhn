# Drug Development Knowledge Library: Biosciences

## Overview

This curated library of authoritative, freely accessible guidance documents supports scientific writing on drug development from discovery through nonclinical studies to CMC (Chemistry, Manufacturing, and Controls). Materials span FDA/ICH harmonized guidelines, regulatory pathways, assay best practices, data integrity standards, and quality systems. All resources listed are available without subscription; most are canonical PDFs or official agency documents.

---

## Nonclinical Safety & IND-Enabling Studies

### ICH M3(R2): Nonclinical Safety Studies for Human Clinical Trials
- **Publisher:** FDA / International Council for Harmonisation (ICH)
- **URL:** https://www.fda.gov/media/71542/download (FDA version)
- **Format:** PDF (Guidance for Industry)
- **Access:** Free, publicly available
- **Description:** Core harmonized guideline defining nonclinical safety study requirements (toxicology, pharmacology, PK) to support IND applications and marketing authorization. Addresses timing, duration, and species selection for safety studies.

### Federal Register Notice: M3(R2) Availability
- **Publisher:** U.S. Federal Register
- **URL:** https://www.federalregister.gov/documents/2010/01/21/2010-1027/international-conference-on-harmonisation-guidance-on-m3r2-nonclinical-safety-studies-for-the-
- **Format:** Federal Register Notice (PDF)
- **Access:** Free, official government record
- **Description:** Formal Federal Register publication of ICH M3(R2) announcement, establishing regulatory basis for the nonclinical safety guidance in the U.S.

### ICH M3(R2) Official Guideline (ICH Database)
- **Publisher:** International Council for Harmonisation (ICH)
- **URL:** https://database.ich.org/sites/default/files/M3_R2__Guideline.pdf
- **Format:** PDF
- **Access:** Free from ICH database
- **Description:** Official Step 4 approved guideline text providing detailed recommendations on nonclinical safety studies, with implementation Q&As for IND-enabling programs.

## Stability Testing & Shelf Life

### ICH Q1A(R2): Stability Testing of Drug Substances and Products
- **Publisher:** FDA
- **URL:** https://www.fda.gov/media/71707/download
- **Format:** PDF (Guidance for Industry)
- **Access:** Free
- **Description:** Core stability testing guidance specifying storage conditions (long-term 25°C/60% RH, intermediate 30°C/65% RH, accelerated 40°C/75% RH), testing intervals, and acceptance criteria for shelf-life determination.

### ICH Q1B: Photostability Testing
- **Publisher:** ICH
- **URL:** https://database.ich.org/sites/default/files/Q1B_Guideline.pdf
- **Format:** PDF
- **Access:** Free from ICH database
- **Description:** Harmonized guidance on light-exposure testing to evaluate photostability of drug substances and products, determining if light-protective packaging or labeling is required.

### ICH Q1E: Evaluation of Stability Data
- **Publisher:** EMA / ICH
- **URL:** https://www.ema.europa.eu/en/ich-q1e-evaluation-stability-data
- **Format:** Scientific guideline (PDF linked)
- **Access:** Free from EMA
- **Description:** Framework for extrapolating stability data beyond the observed period to propose shelf-life and retest periods when regulatory submissions include limited real-time data.

## Notes on Access & Licensing

- All FDA guidance documents listed are official government guidance freely available via FDA.gov and regulatory.gov
- All ICH guidelines are harmonized international standards freely available from the ICH official database
- EMA guidance documents are freely available via the European Medicines Agency website
- NCATS resources are NIH-supported public resources with no paywalls
- Journal author guidelines are publicly available preparation standards, not articles themselves
- OECD GLP principles are available freely through OECD and U.S. government archives
- Some documents may require PDF readers; all are available in portable formats suitable for offline access and citation in technical writing
