# Physics Research Writing: Authoritative Guidance Library

A curated collection of freely accessible, authoritative reference documents for physics research writing, style conventions, uncertainty expression, collaboration practices, and publication standards.

---

## Style & Author Guidance

**APS Journals Style Guide (November 2024)**
- **Publisher/Authority:** American Physical Society (APS)
- **URL:** https://journals.aps.org/authors/style-basics
- **Format:** Web page + PDF
- **Access:** Freely accessible
- **Description:** Comprehensive guidance on formatting, style conventions, text organization, references, language standards, and data availability for all APS Physical Review journals. Includes editorial checklist and manuscript preparation guidelines.

**Physical Review Style and Notation Guide**
- **Publisher/Authority:** American Physical Society (APS)
- **URL:** https://cdn.journals.aps.org/files/styleguide-pr.pdf
- **Format:** PDF
- **Access:** Freely accessible
- **Description:** Detailed reference for style rules, notation conventions, and formatting standards specific to Physical Review journals, including symbol usage and equation preparation.

**AIP Style Manual (4th Edition)**
- **Publisher/Authority:** American Institute of Physics (AIP)
- **URL:** https://publishing.aip.org/wp-content/uploads/2021/03/AIP_Style_4thed.pdf
- **Format:** PDF
- **Access:** Freely accessible
- **Description:** Comprehensive manual for physics manuscript preparation covering planning and writing scientific papers, grammar, punctuation, symbols, units of physical quantities, mathematical expressions, and graph formatting. Standard reference for physics publication submissions.

## SI Units & Measurement Standards

**NIST Special Publication 811: Guide for the Use of the International System of Units (SI)**
- **Publisher/Authority:** National Institute of Standards and Technology (NIST)
- **URL:** https://www.nist.gov/pml/special-publication-811 (DOI: https://doi.org/10.6028/NIST.SP.811e2008)
- **Format:** Web page + PDF (2008 Edition, Version 3.2)
- **Access:** Freely accessible
- **Description:** Official guide to SI unit usage, providing rules for printing, spelling, and expressing physical quantities. Includes SI base and derived unit definitions, conversion factors, and editorial checklist for manuscript review. Authored by A. Thompson and B.N. Taylor.

**Guide to the Expression of Uncertainty in Measurement (GUM) – JCGM 100:2008**
- **Publisher/Authority:** Joint Committee for Guides in Metrology (JCGM) / Bureau International des Poids et Mesures (BIPM)
- **URL:** https://www.bipm.org/documents/20126/2071204/JCGM_100_2008_E.pdf
- **Format:** PDF
- **Access:** Freely accessible
- **Description:** International standard for evaluating and expressing measurement uncertainty in scientific work. Presents general rules applicable across a broad spectrum of measurements, addressing uncertainty evaluation, propagation, and reporting for metrological contexts and physics research.

**NIST Technical Note 1297: Guidelines for Evaluating and Expressing Measurement Uncertainty**
- **Publisher/Authority:** National Institute of Standards and Technology (NIST)
- **URL:** https://www.nist.gov/pml/nist-tn-1297-measurement-uncertainty-1-introduction
- **Format:** Web page + PDF (1994 Edition)
- **Access:** Freely accessible
- **Description:** Practical guide implementing the GUM standard specifically for NIST measurement practices. Prepared by B.N. Taylor and C.E. Kuyatt; widely recognized as valuable for metrologists, scientists, engineers, and statisticians concerned with measurement uncertainty evaluation. Includes introduction, foreword, and detailed sections on uncertainty quantification methods.

## Symbols, Units & Nomenclature Standards

**IUPAP Commission C2 Recommendations: Symbols, Units, Nomenclature and Fundamental Constants**
- **Publisher/Authority:** International Union of Pure and Applied Physics (IUPAP)
- **URL:** https://iupap.org/who-we-are/internal-organization/commissions/c2-commission-on-symbols-units-nomenclature-atomic-masses-and-fundamental-constants/
- **Format:** Web page + reference documents (IUPAP Red Book)
- **Access:** Freely accessible
- **Description:** Authoritative international recommendations on physical quantities symbols, unit notation, nomenclature conventions, and fundamental constants. Specifies that symbols for physical quantities should be printed in italic type while descriptive subscripts and numerical subscripts use roman type. In general agreement with ISO, IUPAC, and BIPM standards.

## Preprint Servers & Submission Guidelines

**arXiv Submission Guidelines**
- **Publisher/Authority:** arXiv (Operated by Cornell University)
- **URL:** https://info.arxiv.org/help/submit/
- **Format:** Web page + linked submission help documents
- **Access:** Freely accessible
- **Description:** Comprehensive submission requirements for physics preprints, including registration and endorsement procedures, format requirements (preferred: LaTeX/PDFLaTeX, acceptable: PDF/HTML), figure format specifications (case-sensitive filenames), technical requirements, moderation policies, and licensing terms. Specifies submission timeline and file naming conventions.

## Data & Code Availability Standards

**APS Physical Review Data Availability Policy**
- **Publisher/Authority:** American Physical Society (APS)
- **URL:** https://journals.aps.org/authors/data-availability-statements
- **Format:** Web page
- **Access:** Freely accessible
- **Description:** Effective policy requiring all Physical Review journal submissions (Dec. 11, 2024 onwards) to include data availability statements. Authors must select pre-scripted statements describing access to research data, code, and materials. Requires justification if data is not publicly shared. Applies to all 17 APS Physical Review journals including Physical Review Letters, PRX, and Physical Review D.

## LaTeX & Document Preparation

**REVTeX 4.2 Author's Guide**
- **Publisher/Authority:** American Physical Society (APS)
- **URL:** https://journals.aps.org/revtex (Documentation: https://ctan.org/tex-archive/macros/latex/contrib/revtex or https://ctan.math.illinois.edu/macros/latex/contrib/revtex/auguide/auguide4-2.pdf)
- **Format:** PDF + Overleaf templates + CTAN repository
- **Access:** Freely accessible
- **Description:** Official LaTeX package for submitting papers to APS, AIP, and OSA journals. Version 4.2 is current, supporting Physical Review Letters, Physical Review A–E, PRX, Physical Review Applied, and other major physics journals. Requirements: natbib (v8.31a+), textcase, bm, AMS-LaTeX. Support available at revtex@aps.org. Includes Overleaf templates and CTAN distribution.

## Large Collaboration & Authorship Conventions

**CERN Author Guidelines**
- **Publisher/Authority:** European Organization for Nuclear Research (CERN)
- **URL:** https://scientific-info.cern/submit-and-publish/author-guidelines
- **Format:** Web page
- **Access:** Freely accessible
- **Description:** Guidance for authors affiliated with CERN and large particle physics collaborations (ATLAS, CMS, etc.). Addresses alphabetical authorship conventions (typical in large experiments with thousands of named authors), collaboration attribution, corresponding author roles, publication approval processes within collaborations, and affiliation formatting. Explains protocols for multi-team collaborative publications and CERN's open access requirements (CC-BY-4.0 licensing).

## Notes on Access

All resources listed are freely accessible without institutional affiliation or subscription requirements. PDF documents can be downloaded directly from publisher or archival URLs. Web pages provide navigation to related resources and submission portals.

For current updates to any guidance document, consult the publisher's official website (APS at journals.aps.org, NIST at nist.gov, BIPM at bipm.org, arXiv at info.arxiv.org, CERN at scientific-info.cern).
