# ML/AI Research Publishing Standards & Best Practices

Authoritative, freely accessible resources for scientific writing standards, reproducibility guidelines, and responsible AI publication norms in machine learning and AI research.

---

## Reproducibility Checklists

### NeurIPS Paper Checklist Guidelines
**Publisher:** Neural Information Processing Systems (NeurIPS) Conference  
**URL:** https://neurips.cc/public/guides/PaperChecklist  
**Format:** Web page + mandatory PDF checklist  
**Access:** Free, public  
Mandatory checklist for all NeurIPS submissions addressing reproducibility, transparency, research ethics, and societal impact—covering claims accuracy, limitations, experimental setup, data/code accessibility, statistical significance, compute requirements, ethics compliance, and LLM usage.

### NeurIPS 2021 Paper Checklist
**Publisher:** NeurIPS Conference  
**URL:** https://neurips.cc/Conferences/2021/PaperInformation/PaperChecklist  
**Format:** Web page with downloadable checklist  
**Access:** Free, public  
Foundational checklist format establishing systematic community-wide approach to reproducibility standards covering methodology, data, code availability, and experimental setup documentation.

### NERVE-ML Checklist (Neural Engineering Reproducibility & Validity Essentials)
**Publisher:** NIH/PMC (published in biomedical engineering journal)  
**URL:** https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC11948487/  
**Format:** Published journal article + checklist  
**Access:** Free, open access (PubMed Central)  
Specialized checklist for ensuring machine learning advances in neural engineering research meet reproducibility and validity standards, adapted for biomedical applications.

## Model Cards & Datasheets for Datasets

### Datasheets for Datasets
**Authors:** Timnit Gebru, Jamie Morgenstern, Briana Vecchione, Jennifer Wortman Vaughan, Hanna Wallach, Hal Daumé III, Kate Crawford  
**Publisher:** arXiv (foundational ML ethics paper)  
**URL:** https://arxiv.org/pdf/1803.09010  
**Format:** arXiv preprint (PDF)  
**Access:** Free, open access  
Foundational framework proposing standardized datasheets accompanying ML datasets, documenting motivation, composition, collection process, preprocessing, ethical considerations, and known limitations.

### Model Cards for Model Reporting
**Authors:** Margaret Mitchell, Simone Wu, Andrew Zaldivar, Parker Barnes, Lucy Vasserman, Ben Hutchinson, Elena Spitzer, Inioluwa Deborah Raji, Timnit Gebru  
**Publisher:** FAT* 2019 conference; republished on arXiv  
**URL:** https://arxiv.org/pdf/1810.03993  
**Format:** arXiv preprint (PDF) / Journal article  
**Access:** Free, open access  
Companion to Datasheets, proposing structured documentation for trained ML models covering type, intended use cases, performance characteristics, demographic breakdowns, and ethical considerations.

## Reporting Standards for ML in Science & Health

### CONSORT-AI: Consolidated Standards of Reporting Trials–Artificial Intelligence
**Lead Authors:** Xiaoxuan Liu, Samantha Cruz Rivera, David Moher, Melanie J Calvert, Alastair K Denniston  
**Publisher:** BMJ (The British Medical Journal), Nature Medicine  
**URL (Open Access):** https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC7490784/  
**URL (Subscription):** https://www.nature.com/articles/s41591-020-1034-x  
**Format:** Journal article + online checklist + supplementary materials  
**Access:** Free (PMC version); subscription (Nature Medicine)  
**Publication Date:** September 9, 2020  
Extension of CONSORT 2010 standards for randomized controlled trials testing AI interventions, adding 14 new checklist items addressing algorithm versioning, data handling, human-AI interaction, and performance error analysis.

### SPIRIT-AI: Standard Protocol Items for AI Intervention Trial Protocols
**Lead Authors:** Hussein Ibrahim, Xiaoxuan Liu, Samantha Cruz Rivera, David Moher, An-Wen Chan, Matthew R Sydes, Melanie J Calvert, Alastair K Denniston  
**Publisher:** Trials journal (Springer Nature), Nature Medicine  
**URL (Open Access):** https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC7788716/  
**URL (Subscription):** https://www.nature.com/articles/s41591-020-1037-7  
**Format:** Journal article + protocol checklist  
**Access:** Free (PMC version); subscription (Nature Medicine)  
**Publication Date:** January 6, 2021  
Extension of SPIRIT 2013 standards for clinical trial protocols involving AI interventions, establishing 15 new reporting items for trial protocol design addressing AI-specific elements like algorithm versions and bias assessment.

### STARD-AI: Standards for Reporting of Diagnostic Accuracy–Artificial Intelligence
**Lead Authors:** Viknesh Sounderajah, Ahmad Guni, Xiaoxuan Liu, Gary S Collins, and collaborators  
**Publisher:** Nature Medicine  
**URL:** https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/40954311/  
**Format:** Journal article + 52-item checklist  
**Access:** Subscription (Nature Medicine); check PubMed Central for open access version  
**Publication Date:** October 2025 (online September 15, 2025)  
AI extension of STARD 2015 diagnostic accuracy reporting standards, comprehensively addressing dataset practices, AI evaluation methodology, algorithmic bias, fairness assessment, and generalizability for diagnostic AI studies.

### CLAIM: Checklist for Artificial Intelligence in Medical Imaging
**Authors (2024 Update):** Ali S. Tejani, Michail E. Klontzas, Anthony A. Gatti, John T. Mongan, Linda Moy, Seong Ho Park, Charles E. Kahn Jr.  
**Publisher:** Radiology: Artificial Intelligence (RSNA—Radiological Society of North America)  
**URL (Original 2020):** https://pubs.rsna.org/doi/full/10.1148/ryai.2020200029  
**URL (Updated 2024):** https://pubs.rsna.org/doi/full/10.1148/ryai.240300  
**Format:** Journal article + 44-item checklist  
**Access:** Open access (RSNA)  
44-item checklist (revised 2024) for transparent and reproducible reporting of medical imaging AI research, organized by paper sections with response options and requirement for evidence documentation.

### TRIPOD+AI: Transparent Reporting of Multivariable Prediction Model (In Development)
**Publisher:** EQUATOR Network  
**Status:** Work in progress  
**Format:** Expected checklist format (similar to TRIPOD, CONSORT, SPIRIT)  
**Access:** Will be open access (EQUATOR standards)  
AI extension of TRIPOD standards for reporting prediction and prognostic models using AI, addressing dataset documentation, model development methodology, validation approaches, and bias/fairness assessment.

## Major Conference Author Guides & Policies

### ICLR 2026 Author Guide
**Publisher:** International Conference on Learning Representations (ICLR)  
**URL:** https://iclr.cc/Conferences/2026/AuthorGuide  
**Format:** Web page with LaTeX template links  
**Access:** Free, public  
Comprehensive author guidelines covering formatting (9-page limit, 10 camera-ready, LaTeX mandatory), anonymity, LLM disclosure requirements (must disclose significant contribution to ideation/writing), code submission, and ethics requirements.

### ICLR 2026 LLM Usage Policies
**Publisher:** ICLR  
**URL:** https://blog.iclr.cc/2025/08/26/policies-on-large-language-model-usage-at-iclr-2026/  
**Format:** Blog post + formal policy  
**Access:** Free, public  
Mandatory disclosure of all LLM use in papers and submission forms, with author verification of accuracy required; use in ideation/code generation/analysis requires disclosure; hidden "prompt injections" in reviews constitute research misconduct.

### NeurIPS 2025 LLM Usage Policy
**Publisher:** Neural Information Processing Systems (NeurIPS) Conference  
**URL:** https://neurips.cc/Conferences/2025/LLM  
**Format:** Official policy page  
**Access:** Free, public  
Permissive LLM policy allowing all tools suitable for high-quality paper preparation; no disclosure required for grammar/spell-checking; must disclose use as "important, original, or non-standard" methodology component; only humans can be authors; reviewers cannot share submissions with LLMs.

### ICML 2025 Publication Ethics Guidelines
**Publisher:** International Conference on Machine Learning (ICML)  
**URL:** https://icml.cc/Conferences/2025/PublicationEthics  
**Format:** Official ethics guidelines  
**Access:** Free, public  
Comprehensive ethics framework requiring discrimination/bias/fairness risk assessment, thoughtful discussion of inappropriate applications (weapons, surveillance), IRB approval for human subjects research, privacy/security safeguards, legal compliance (EU AI Act), and originality standards.

### ICML 2026 LLM Policies (Dual-Policy Framework)
**Publisher:** ICML  
**URL:** https://icml.cc/Conferences/2026/Intro-LLM-Policy  
**Format:** Official policy page  
**Access:** Free, public  
Innovative dual-policy framework allowing researchers to choose LLM comfort level: Policy A (conservative, prohibits LLM review use) or Policy B (permissive, allows comprehension and language refinement); authors declare preference; system ensures reviewer alignment.

## Benchmark & Evaluation Standards

### ML Systems Textbook: Benchmarking Chapter
**Publisher:** MIT Press / Open educational resource  
**URL:** https://mlsysbook.ai/book/contents/core/benchmarking/benchmarking.html  
**Format:** Web-based textbook chapter  
**Access:** Free, open access  
Comprehensive treatment of ML benchmarking principles covering standardized evaluation frameworks, multi-dimensional assessment (accuracy + robustness + cost), diverse datasets, public code, and modern standards for fairness, interpretability, and environmental impact.

### Code Benchmarks Should Prioritize Rigor, Reliability, and Reproducibility
**Publisher:** arXiv preprint  
**URL:** https://arxiv.org/pdf/2501.10711  
**Format:** Research paper  
**Access:** Free, open access  
Research paper emphasizing methodological rigor in code benchmark development with focus on reliability and reproducibility as foundational requirements, advancing benchmarking standards toward transparency and trustworthiness.

### Integrating Machine Learning Standards in Disseminating ML Research
**Publisher:** Data Science Journal (CODATA)  
**URL:** https://datascience.codata.org/articles/10.5334/dsj-2026-001  
**Format:** Journal article  
**Access:** Open access  
Analysis of integration of ML standards across publication workflows, addressing how to mainstream benchmarking, reproducibility, and reporting standards in ML research dissemination.

## arXiv Submission Conventions

### Official arXiv Submission Guide
**Publisher:** arXiv.org (Cornell University eprint archive)  
**URL:** https://info.arxiv.org/help/submit/index.html  
**Format:** Official documentation website  
**Access:** Free, public  
Official guidelines covering registration, format requirements (TeX/LaTeX preferred; PDF second choice), figure specifications, file naming conventions, moderation policies, submission timeline, and quality checks for preprint submission.

### arXiv Preprints: A Practical Guide
**Publisher:** CASRAI (Consortium Advancing Standards in Research Administration Information)  
**URL:** https://casrai.org/guides/arxiv-preprints-what-they-are-and-how-to-use-them  
**Format:** Web guide  
**Access:** Free, public  
Practical guide covering what arXiv preprints are, appropriate uses, strategic timing for establishing priority claims, and best practices for gathering community feedback.

### A Checklist for Submitting to arXiv
**Publisher:** Towards Data Science  
**URL:** https://towardsdatascience.com/a-checklist-for-submitting-your-research-to-arxiv-64f31b4127d2/  
**Format:** Article with downloadable checklist  
**Access:** Free, public  
Practical checklist for common arXiv submission mistakes including figure format compatibility, file naming conventions, citation verification, and quality assurance steps.

## Responsible AI Publication Norms

### Publication Norms for Responsible AI
**Publisher:** Partnership on AI  
**URL:** https://partnershiponai.org/workstream/publication-norms-for-responsible-ai/  
**White Paper:** https://arxiv.org/pdf/2009.07262  
**Format:** White paper + organizational guidance  
**Access:** Free, open access  
Comprehensive guidance maximizing AI research benefits while mitigating harms, addressing timing, scope, audience, and risk assessment for all research dissemination decisions—covering individual researchers, research leadership, and conference/journal practices with alternative release strategies (delayed, staged, timed, periodic evaluation, audience restrictions).

### AI Ethics Statements: Analysis and Lessons Learnt from NeurIPS Broader Impact Statements
**Publisher:** arXiv preprint (meta-analysis of conference practices)  
**URL:** https://arxiv.org/pdf/2111.01705  
**Format:** Research paper  
**Access:** Free, open access  
Analysis of NeurIPS broader impact statements from 2020-2021, evaluating how this requirement succeeded in surfacing ethical concerns with recommendations for improving responsible AI publication practices at scale.

### NeurIPS Broader Impacts Evolution
**Publisher:** Neural Information Processing Systems  
Broader impacts requirement introduced 2020, modified to checklist format 2021—encouraging researchers to critically assess research effects (positive and negative) on vulnerable populations and potential misuse scenarios.

### ICLR Code of Ethics
**Publisher:** International Conference on Learning Representations  
Incorporates ethics review into peer review process, making responsible research practices mainstream consideration at top ML conferences.
