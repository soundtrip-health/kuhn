# Social Sciences Research Writing: Knowledge Library

A curated collection of authoritative, freely accessible guidance documents and standards for scientific writing in the social sciences. Resources cover reporting guidelines, ethics, open science practices, data management, and research methodology across quantitative, qualitative, and mixed-methods approaches.

---

## General Reporting Standards & Frameworks

### EQUATOR Network — Reporting Guidelines Catalog
- **Publisher/Authority:** EQUATOR (Enhancing the QUAlity and Transparency Of Health Research)
- **URL:** https://www.equator-network.org/reporting-guidelines/
- **Format:** HTML; searchable database of 700+ guidelines
- **Access/License:** Freely accessible; most guidelines available as downloadable PDFs
- **Description:** Centralized repository of 700+ research reporting guidelines covering diverse study designs (RCTs, observational studies, qualitative research, systematic reviews, diagnostic accuracy studies) and clinical specialties. Includes checklists, elaboration documents, and guidance materials to enhance transparency and quality.

### Transparency and Openness Promotion (TOP) Guidelines
- **Publisher/Authority:** Center for Open Science (COS)
- **URL:** https://www.cos.io/initiatives/top-guidelines
- **Format:** Preprint + published article
- **Access/License:** Freely accessible; full preprint on OSF Preprints (CC BY 4.0)
- **Description:** Policy framework advancing open science by establishing eight standards (data citation, data transparency, analytic methods transparency, research materials transparency, reporting guidelines, preregistration, preregistration of analysis plans, replication) with three levels of implementation stringency. Updated in 2025.

## Quantitative Research Reporting

### APA Style Journal Article Reporting Standards (JARS)
- **Publisher/Authority:** American Psychological Association
- **URL:** https://apastyle.apa.org/jars
- **Format:** HTML with downloadable PDFs; includes interactive modules and flowcharts
- **Access/License:** Freely accessible
- **Description:** Comprehensive guidance for reporting quantitative (JARS-Quant), qualitative (JARS-Qual), mixed-methods (JARS-Mixed), and culture-sensitive research (JARS-REC). Includes decision flowcharts, participant flow templates, FAQs, and educational resources (webinars, podcasts, videos).

### CONSORT Statement — Randomized Controlled Trials
- **Publisher/Authority:** CONSORT Collaboration
- **URL:** https://www.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/pmc/articles/PMC2857832/ (2010 update)
- **Format:** PDF; 25-item checklist + flowchart + explanation/elaboration article
- **Access/License:** Freely accessible (PMC open access)
- **Description:** Consolidated Standards of Reporting Trials (CONSORT) for RCTs with a 25-item checklist and participant flow diagram. Published 2010, with 2025 update recently released; endorsed by leading journals and over 5,000 signatories.

### STROBE Statement — Observational Studies
- **Publisher/Authority:** STROBE Collaboration
- **URL:** https://www.strobe-statement.org/
- **Format:** PDF and Word checklists; multilingual versions
- **Access/License:** Freely accessible; open-access publication
- **Description:** STrengthening the Reporting of OBservational Studies in Epidemiology (STROBE) — 22-item checklist for observational research (cohort, case-control, cross-sectional studies) with study-design-specific variants. Includes explanation/elaboration articles and conference abstracts guidance.

### American Statistical Association Statement on P-Values
- **Publisher/Authority:** American Statistical Association (ASA)
- **URL:** https://www.stat.berkeley.edu/~aldous/Real_World/ASA_statement.pdf (full article PDF)
- **Format:** PDF article with commentaries
- **Access/License:** Freely accessible
- **Description:** Comprehensive guidance on the proper interpretation and use of p-values in statistical testing, addressing misinterpretations and contextualizing p-values within broader research and causal inference frameworks.

## Qualitative Research Reporting

### COREQ — Consolidated Criteria for Reporting Qualitative Research
- **Publisher/Authority:** EQUATOR Network; Tong et al.
- **URL:** https://www.equator-network.org/reporting-guidelines/coreq/
- **Format:** 32-item checklist (PDF, Word); explanation/elaboration article
- **Access/License:** Freely accessible
- **Description:** 32-item checklist designed for interview and focus group studies, organized into three domains (research team and reflexivity, study design, data analysis and reporting). Widely adopted in health and social sciences with 30,000+ citations.

### SRQR — Standards for Reporting Qualitative Research
- **Publisher/Authority:** EQUATOR Network
- **URL:** https://www.equator-network.org/reporting-guidelines-study-design/qualitative-research/
- **Format:** 21-item checklist (PDF, Word); explanation/elaboration article
- **Access/License:** Freely accessible
- **Description:** Standards for Reporting Qualitative Research (SRQR) — 21-item checklist for any qualitative study design (grounded theory, phenomenology, ethnography, thematic analysis) spanning problem formulation through dissemination. Discipline-agnostic and endorsed by EQUATOR.

- **Publisher/Authority:** PRISMA Collaboration
- **URL:** https://www.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/pmc/articles/PMC8005924/ (PRISMA 2020)
- **Format:** 27-item checklist + flowchart; explanation/elaboration article (PMC open access)

## Research Ethics & Human Subjects Protection

### The Belmont Report
- **Publisher/Authority:** U.S. National Commission for the Protection of Human Subjects of Biomedical and Behavioral Research
- **URL:** https://history.nih.gov/display/history/Belmont+Report (NIH History); https://archive.org/details/belmontreporteth00unit (Internet Archive)
- **Format:** PDF/full text
- **Access/License:** Public domain; freely accessible
- **Description:** Foundational 1978 ethical framework establishing three core principles (Respect for Persons, Beneficence, Justice) and their applications (informed consent, risk/benefit assessment, equitable subject selection) for human-subjects research. Basis for all IRB review protocols.

### Federal Policy for the Protection of Human Subjects (Common Rule)
- **Publisher/Authority:** U.S. Department of Health & Human Services (HHS Office for Human Research Protections)
- **URL:** https://www.hhs.gov/ohrp/regulations-and-policy/regulations/common-rule/index.html
- **Format:** HTML regulations + related guidance
- **Access/License:** Freely accessible
- **Description:** Federal regulation (45 CFR Part 46) codifying the Belmont Report's principles into binding requirements across federal agencies. Covers IRB composition, informed consent procedures, risk assessment, and special protections for vulnerable populations.

### Fostering Integrity in Research
- **Publisher/Authority:** National Academies of Sciences, Engineering, and Medicine
- **URL:** https://nap.nationalacademies.org/read/21896/chapter/1
- **Format:** Full book available online (HTML)
- **Access/License:** Freely accessible
- **Description:** 2017 consensus report identifying best practices and practical options for addressing research misconduct and detrimental practices. Updates 25-year-old National Academies guidance to reflect evolving understanding of research integrity in modern contexts.

## Data Sharing & Metadata Standards

### FAIR Data Principles
- **Publisher/Authority:** Data4LifeSciences initiative (international consortium)
- **URL:** https://data.org/resources/the-fair-data-principles/
- **Format:** HTML with explanation and implementation guidance
- **Access/License:** Freely accessible
- **Description:** Four foundational principles for research data stewardship: Findable (persistent identifiers and rich metadata), Accessible (retrievable via standardized protocols), Interoperable (uses standard vocabularies), and Reusable (citable, well-documented). Does not require data to be open, but requires metadata to remain discoverable.

### Data Documentation Initiative (DDI) Standard
- **Publisher/Authority:** Data Documentation Initiative Alliance (international standards body)
- **URL:** https://www.ddialliance.org/
- **Format:** XML specification + documentation
- **Access/License:** Open standard, freely accessible
- **Description:** International standard for describing data from surveys and observational studies in social, behavioral, economic, and health sciences. Supports entire research data lifecycle and is adopted by major archives including ICPSR and repositories worldwide.

### ICPSR Data Archiving & Deposit Guide
- **Publisher/Authority:** Inter-university Consortium for Political and Social Research (ICPSR), University of Michigan
- **URL:** https://www.icpsr.umich.edu/web/pages/deposit/
- **Format:** HTML guides + documentation
- **Access/License:** Freely accessible
- **Description:** Leadership resource for data curation and archiving in social science research. Provides guidance on data preparation, metadata standards (DDI, DCAT, Dublin Core, MARC), and best practices for ensuring data access, reusability, and compliance with FAIR principles.

## Mixed Methods Research

### Mixed Methods Appraisal Tool (MMAT)
- **Publisher/Authority:** National Collaborating Centre for Methods and Tools (NCCMT); Hong, Pluye, et al.
- **URL:** https://www.nccmt.ca/resources/search/232 (User Guide); http://mixedmethodsappraisaltoolpublic.pbworks.com/ (public wiki)
- **Format:** PDF checklist + tutorial + FAQ
- **Access/License:** Freely available for education and research
- **Description:** Quality appraisal tool for critically evaluating quantitative, qualitative, and mixed-methods studies in systematic reviews. Provides screening questions and overall quality scores to assess methodological rigor across study types.

## Common Data Elements & Standardized Measures

### PhenX Toolkit — Social Determinants of Health Measures
- **Publisher/Authority:** National Institute on Minority Health and Health Disparities (NIMHD), NIH
- **URL:** https://www.phenxtoolkit.org/ (main site); https://www.phenxtoolkit.org/collections/sdoh (SDoH collection)
- **Format:** Interactive web-based catalog with downloadable protocols
- **Access/License:** Freely accessible
- **Description:** Catalog of recommended, consensus-based measurement protocols for phenotypes and exposures in social science and health research. The SDoH collection includes 19+ protocols for social determinants of health research to enable cross-study data harmonization and comparison.
