# Robotics Research Writing: Knowledge Library

Authoritative, freely-accessible guidance for authors publishing robotics research. Includes IEEE editorial standards, conference author kits, reproducibility frameworks, evaluation benchmarks, and technical documentation conventions.

---

## IEEE Editorial Style & Manuscript Preparation

**IEEE Editorial Style Manual**
- Publisher/Authority: IEEE (Institute of Electrical and Electronics Engineers)
- URL: https://www.ieee-ras.org/publications/ra-l/ra-l-information-for-authors/ (via IEEE Author Center)
- Format: PDF, online reference
- Access: Open access via IEEE Author Center; templates and guidelines freely available
- Description: Comprehensive editorial guidelines for IEEE technical publications, covering citation styles, formatting conventions, and manuscript structure for robotics and engineering research.

**IEEE Author Center & Templates**
- Publisher/Authority: IEEE
- URL: https://www.ieee.org/publications-standards/publications/authors/index.html
- Format: Online repository with LaTeX and Word templates
- Access: Free; downloadable templates for all IEEE robotics journals and conferences
- Description: Central repository for IEEE article preparation templates, style files, and submission tools supporting both LaTeX and Microsoft Word manuscript preparation.

## Conference Author Kits: ICRA, IROS, RSS

**ICRA 2026: Final Paper Submission Instructions**
- Publisher/Authority: IEEE International Conference on Robotics and Automation
- URL: https://2026.ieee-icra.org/contribute/final-paper-submission-instructions/
- Format: Web-based submission guidelines, PDF specifications
- Access: Open access
- Description: Specifies 8-page limit (including all figures, tables, and references), IEEE double-column format, and mandatory AI-generated content screening for the flagship robotics conference; papers exceeding limits are summarily rejected.

**IROS 2025/2026: Paper Submission Guidelines**
- Publisher/Authority: IEEE/RSJ International Conference on Intelligent Robots and Systems
- URL: https://www.iros25.org/FinalSubmissions and https://2026.ieee-iros.org/contribute/call-for-papers/
- Format: Web-based submission portal and guidelines
- Access: Open access
- Description: Requires 6-page maximum papers (plus 2 optional extra pages) in double-column IEEE format via PaperPlaza; mandates author anonymization and discloses AI usage policies for generative tools in acknowledgments.

**RSS 2025: Author Information & Submission Requirements**
- Publisher/Authority: Robotics: Science and Systems
- URL: https://roboticsconference.org/2025/information/authorinfo/
- Format: Web-based guidelines and templates
- Access: Open access
- Description: Requires template-specific submissions with mandatory "Limitations" section, strict author anonymization during double-blind review, and careful self-citation management; papers submitted in non-standard formats are desk-rejected.

## IEEE Robotics Journals: Author Guidelines

**IEEE Robotics and Automation Letters (RA-L): Information for Authors**
- Publisher/Authority: IEEE Robotics and Automation Society
- URL: https://www.ieee-ras.org/publications/ra-l/ra-l-information-for-authors/
- Format: Web-based submission guidelines and PDF checklist
- Access: Open access
- Description: Mandates 6-page maximum letters in IEEE two-column format (expandable to 8 pages with fees); guarantees first editorial decision within 3 months; requires double-anonymous review and submission via IEEE Author Portal.

**IEEE Robotics & Automation Magazine (RAM): Reproducible Article Guidelines**
- Publisher/Authority: IEEE Robotics and Automation Society
- URL: https://www.ieee-ras.org/publications/ram/information-for-authors/
- Format: Web-based submission guidelines
- Access: Open access
- Description: R-article track requires complete code identifiers, dataset deposits (Code Ocean/GitHub), hardware descriptions, and statistical measures for reproducibility; includes supplemental data/code consistency review pre-publication.

**IEEE Transactions on Robotics (T-RO): Information for Authors**
- Publisher/Authority: IEEE Robotics and Automation Society
- URL: https://ieeexplore.ieee.org/document/10478166
- Format: IEEE Xplore journal portal
- Access: Subscription required for full text; author guidelines accessible
- Description: Publishes fundamental papers on all robotics aspects; follows IEEE manuscript preparation standards and editorial review processes for longer-form contributions than letters.

**IEEE Transactions on Field Robotics (T-FR): Information for Authors**
- Publisher/Authority: IEEE Robotics and Automation Society
- URL: https://www.ieee-ras.org/publications/t-fr/information-for-authors
- Format: Web-based submission guidelines
- Access: Open access
- Description: Emphasizes experimental validation in field environments or appropriate analogs; simulation-only results are insufficient; manuscripts must support empirical evidence and practical significance claims.

## Reproducibility Standards & Best Practices

**IEEE RAS Seasonal School: Reproducible Research, Performance Evaluation & Benchmarking**
- Publisher/Authority: IEEE Robotics and Automation Society
- URL: https://www.ieee-ras.org/component/rseventspro/event/2104-ieee-ras-seasonal-school-on-reproducible-research-performance-evaluation-and-benchmarking-in-robotics
- Format: Educational workshop materials and guidance
- Access: Open access to event information and published materials
- Description: Authoritative training on reproducible research practices in robotics, covering experimental protocols, benchmarking methodologies, and standardized reporting formats for robotic validation campaigns.

**MIRRER Framework: Multiple Iterated, Reproduced, and Replicated Experiments with Robots**
- Publisher/Authority: National Science Foundation & NIST-sponsored research
- URL: https://arxiv.org/html/2408.04736
- Format: Peer-reviewed ArXiv paper
- Access: Open access (arXiv preprint)
- Description: Conceptual framework unifying reproducibility, replicability, and iterative evaluation to address challenges in robotic research evaluation; includes standards (ASTM F3218-19, F3381-19) for environment and object characterization.

**SwarmRob: Toolkit for Reproducibility and Sharing of Experimental Artifacts**
- Publisher/Authority: Robotics research community
- URL: https://arxiv.org/pdf/1801.04199
- Format: ArXiv paper describing open-source toolkit
- Access: Open access
- Description: Software toolkit enabling reproducible robotics research through standardized artifact sharing, dataset management, and experiment protocol documentation for multi-robot and swarm applications.

## Benchmarking & Evaluation Standards

**Benchmarking Protocols for Evaluating Small Parts Robotic Assembly Systems**
- Publisher/Authority: National Institute of Standards and Technology (NIST)
- URL: https://www.nist.gov/publications/benchmarking-protocols-evaluating-small-parts-robotic-assembly-systems
- Format: PDF technical document
- Access: Open access
- Description: NIST-developed standardized protocols and task boards for evaluating robotic manipulation in assembly tasks; includes peg-in-hole benchmarks, force-based performance metrics, and comparison methodologies for gripper and control strategies.

**Benchmarking Protocols for Robotic Manipulation: Special Issue (RA-L)**
- Publisher/Authority: IEEE Robotics and Automation Society
- URL: https://www.ieee-ras.org/publications/ra-l/special-issues/past-special-issues/benchmarking-protocols-for-robotic-manipulation/
- Format: Special journal issue (RA-L)
- Access: Papers open access via RA-L
- Description: Curated collection of papers establishing standardized evaluation metrics and protocols for robotic manipulation research, including task success rates, cycle time, precision, and multi-layered outcome measures.

**Task-Driven SLAM Benchmarking for Robot Navigation**
- Publisher/Authority: Robotics research community
- URL: https://arxiv.org/abs/2409.16573
- Format: ArXiv preprint
- Access: Open access
- Description: Proposes task-driven benchmarking approach for SLAM algorithms using precision metrics and mapping capability assessment; standardizes Absolute Trajectory Error (ATE) and Relative Pose Error (RPE) as canonical evaluation metrics.

**MRPB 1.0: Unified Benchmark for Mobile Robot Local Planning**
- Publisher/Authority: Robotics research community
- URL: https://arxiv.org/pdf/2011.00491
- Format: ArXiv PDF
- Access: Open access
- Description: Establishes unified benchmarking framework for evaluating mobile robot local planning approaches across multiple environments and scenarios with standardized metrics and performance comparison protocols.

## Robotics Vocabulary & Terminology Standards

**ISO 8373:2021 – Robotics: Vocabulary**
- Publisher/Authority: International Organization for Standardization (ISO)
- URL: https://www.iso.org/standard/75539.html
- Format: Standards document (paywalled official; free sample available)
- Access: Official standard requires purchase (~$250 USD); free sample preview and summary available through ISO website; Edition 3 published November 2021
- Description: International standard defining standardized terminology for robotics covering mechanical structure, kinematics, programming, control, performance, sensing, navigation, and modularity across industrial and non-industrial applications.

## ROS Documentation & Code Standards

**ROS 2 Developer Guide**
- Publisher/Authority: Open Robotics Foundation
- URL: https://docs.ros.org/en/rolling/The-ROS2-Project/Contributing/Developer-Guide.html
- Format: Web-based documentation
- Access: Open access
- Description: Comprehensive developer conventions covering C++ style guidelines (<=120 character lines, Doxygen documentation), CMake standards, package naming (lowercase, underscores only), and code documentation requirements using auto-generated API references.

**ROS C++ Style Guide & ros_lint**
- Publisher/Authority: ROS community (Industrial Training resources)
- URL: https://industrial-training-master.readthedocs.io/en/melodic/_source/session6/Style-Guide-and-ros_lint.html
- Format: Web-based tutorial and reference
- Access: Open access
- Description: Establishes C++ coding standards for ROS packages, including line length limits, comment formatting, alphabetized dependencies, and ros_lint tool for automated style validation.

**ROS Best Practices Wiki**
- Publisher/Authority: Legged Robotics lab (ETH Zurich)
- URL: https://github.com/leggedrobotics/ros_best_practices/wiki
- Format: GitHub wiki with code examples
- Access: Open access
- Description: Curated best practices for ROS package structure, testing, documentation, and development workflow; includes recommendations for package-level documentation with topics, services, parameters, and launch file specifications.

## Sim-to-Real Transfer & Experiment Reporting

**The Reality Gap in Robotics: Challenges, Solutions, and Best Practices**
- Publisher/Authority: Annual Reviews
- URL: https://www.annualreviews.org/content/journals/10.1146/annurev-control-031924-100130
- Format: Peer-reviewed review article
- Access: Subscription required; open-access version available via ArXiv
- Description: Comprehensive review of sim-to-real transfer challenges and solutions, covering domain randomization, real-to-sim approaches, and best practices for reporting experimental results with statistical measures (bootstrapped standard deviation across ≥10 trials).

**Sim-to-Real Transfer in Deep Reinforcement Learning for Robotics: A Survey**
- Publisher/Authority: Robotics research community
- URL: https://arxiv.org/pdf/2009.13303
- Format: ArXiv survey paper
- Access: Open access
- Description: Systematic survey of sim-to-real transfer techniques including hardware setup documentation, perceptual input specification, baseline comparisons, and standardized evaluation protocols for robotic learning systems.

**Reconciling Reality Through Simulation: Real-to-Sim-to-Real Approach for Manipulation**
- Publisher/Authority: Robotics research community
- URL: https://arxiv.org/html/2403.03949v1
- Format: ArXiv paper with code
- Access: Open access
- Description: Demonstrates bidirectional sim-real validation methodology for robotic manipulation, establishing best practices for documenting simulation environments, controller parameters, and experimental procedures enabling reproducible transfer.

**RLBench: Robot Learning Benchmark and Learning Environment**
- Publisher/Authority: Robotics research community
- URL: Available via academic repositories (ArXiv and GitHub)
- Format: Open-source benchmark platform (Python)
- Access: Open source (MIT license)
- Description: Standardized benchmark and simulation environment for robot learning research, enabling controlled evaluation of manipulation policies with consistent task definitions, metrics, and sim-to-real validation protocols.
