# Chemistry Research Writing Knowledge Library

Authoritative, freely accessible guidance for chemistry publication standards, compound characterization, and reporting requirements.

---

## ACS Style & Author Guidelines

**ACS Author Lab: Manuscript Preparation Course**
- **Authority**: American Chemical Society
- **URL**: https://researcher-resources.acs.org/publish/author_guidelines
- **Format**: Web-based interactive course
- **Access/License**: Free online training
- **Description**: Comprehensive guidance on preparing and submitting manuscripts to ACS journals, covering formatting standards, citation style, and best practices for scientific communication.

**ACS Journal Manuscript Submission Guide**
- **Authority**: American Chemical Society
- **URL**: https://pubs.acs.org/page/4authors/submission_guide
- **Format**: Web documentation
- **Access/License**: Free online
- **Description**: Standardized formatting requirements and submission procedures applicable across all ACS journals, including expectations for compound characterization data and supporting information.

**ACS NMR Guidelines (2013)**
- **Authority**: American Chemical Society
- **URL**: https://pubsapp.acs.org/paragonplus/submission/acs_nmr_guidelines.pdf
- **Format**: PDF
- **Access/License**: Free download
- **Description**: Detailed standards for reporting NMR spectroscopy data in ACS publications, specifying chemical shift precision, coupling constants, nucleus identification, and submission of FID/processing parameters.

## IUPAC Nomenclature & Standards

**IUPAC Color Books (Collective)**
- **Authority**: International Union of Pure and Applied Chemistry
- **URL**: https://iupac.org/what-we-do/books/color-books/
- **Format**: PDF (most editions available free); print (purchasable)
- **Access/License**: Older editions free; current editions available from IUPAC/RSC
- **Description**: Eight foundational references covering chemical nomenclature (Blue Book: organic; Red Book: inorganic), units and terminology (Green Book), polymer nomenclature (Purple Book), analytical terminology (Orange Book), and biochemistry (White Book). The Gold Book provides online interactive chemical terminology.

**IUPAC Gold Book (Chemical Terminology)**
- **Authority**: International Union of Pure and Applied Chemistry
- **URL**: https://goldbook.iupac.org/
- **Format**: Interactive web database
- **Access/License**: Free online access
- **Description**: Comprehensive online dictionary of chemistry terms with standardized definitions, chemical structures, and pronunciation guides; ideal for ensuring consistent terminology in publications.

**IUPAC InChI Standard**
- **Authority**: International Union of Pure and Applied Chemistry
- **URL**: https://iupac.org/who-we-are/divisions/division-details/inchi/
- **Format**: Standard specification + software
- **Access/License**: Free downloads, open standard
- **Description**: Official specification for the IUPAC International Chemical Identifier (InChI), a non-proprietary character-string identifier for unambiguous chemical structure representation across databases and publications.

## Spectroscopy & Compound Characterization Standards

**NMReDATA: NMR Assignment & Parameters Standard**
- **Authority**: International Union of Pure and Applied Chemistry + Mestrelabs
- **URL**: https://nmredatainitiative.github.io/
- **Format**: Structured text format (extension of MOL/SDF)
- **Access/License**: Free standard, open-source tools
- **Description**: Standardized format for reporting NMR chemical shifts, multiplicities, coupling constants, 2D correlations, and atom assignments in organic compounds; human- and machine-readable, enabling automated verification of structural assignments.

**nmrML: Open XML Format for NMR Data**
- **Authority**: Metabolomics Standards Initiative / ACS Publications
- **URL**: https://pubs.acs.org/doi/full/10.1021/acs.analchem.7b02795
- **Format**: XML schema + converters
- **Access/License**: Open standard, endorsed by MSI; published open-access 2018
- **Description**: Vendor-agnostic XML format for archiving raw NMR data, acquisition parameters, and metadata; compatible with metabolomics repositories (MetaboLights); converters available for Bruker, JEOL, Agilent/Varian instruments.

**NMR File Formats & Documentation**
- **Authority**: nmrXiv (community NMR data platform)
- **URL**: https://docs.nmrxiv.org/introduction/data/formats.html
- **Format**: Web documentation
- **Access/License**: Free reference
- **Description**: Practical guide comparing NMR data formats (nmrML, NMReDATA, vendor formats) and tools for format conversion, spectral processing, and data sharing.

**High-Resolution Mass Spectrometry (HRMS) Reporting**
- **Authority**: ACS & Journal of the American Society for Mass Spectrometry
- **URL**: https://www.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/pmc/articles/PMC12412142/
- **Format**: Peer-reviewed article (open access)
- **Access/License**: Free full-text
- **Description**: Standards for mass accuracy, system suitability testing, calibration procedures, and documentation of high-resolution mass spectrometry results for small-molecule characterization; addresses accuracy requirements for nontarget and suspect screening.

**Elemental Analysis (CHN) Reporting Standards**
- **Authority**: ACS Central Science (peer-reviewed)
- **URL**: https://www.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/pmc/articles/PMC9335909/
- **Format**: Peer-reviewed article (open access)
- **Access/License**: Free full-text
- **Description**: Guidelines for carbon/hydrogen/nitrogen combustion analysis reporting; notes industry-standard ±0.4% deviation requirement and statistical analysis of achievable precision across international service providers.

**Compound Purity Determination & Reporting**
- **Authority**: ACS Journal of Medicinal Chemistry
- **URL**: https://pubs.acs.org/doi/10.1021/jm801525s
- **Format**: Journal article
- **Access/License**: Published standard
- **Description**: Requirements for tested compounds (≥95% purity); documentation of HPLC, qNMR, or DSC methods; specification of purity percentage when <95%; note that spectral data alone is insufficient for purity qualification.

## Crystallography Standards & Tools

**Crystallographic Information Framework (CIF)**
- **Authority**: International Union of Crystallography
- **URL**: https://www.iucr.org/resources/cif
- **Format**: Standard specification + documentation
- **Access/License**: Free specification and reference documents
- **Description**: Standardized text file format for archiving and distributing crystal structure data; adopted in 1990 and now required for publication in Acta Crystallographica and most structural journals; contains unit cell parameters, atomic coordinates, thermal parameters, and symmetry information.

**CheckCIF Validation Service**
- **Authority**: International Union of Crystallography
- **URL**: https://checkcif.iucr.org/
- **Format**: Web service
- **Access/License**: Free online validation
- **Description**: Pre-publication validation tool for CIF files that checks syntax, data consistency, geometry/symmetry using PLATON software, and screens for potential structural errors; generates HTML or PDF reports at multiple alert levels.

**CIF Syntax Guide**
- **Authority**: Cambridge Crystallographic Data Centre
- **URL**: https://www.ccdc.cam.ac.uk/media/MoreInformationAboutCIFsyntax.pdf
- **Format**: PDF reference
- **Access/License**: Free download
- **Description**: Practical guide to CIF file construction, formatting rules, and best practices for crystal structure data reporting; includes examples and common errors to avoid.

**Cambridge Structural Database (CSD)**
- **Authority**: Cambridge Crystallographic Data Centre
- **URL**: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Cambridge_Structural_Database
- **Format**: Searchable database + web interface
- **Access/License**: Free community web services; institutional subscriptions for full access
- **Description**: Repository of ~700,000 small-molecule crystal structures with CIF submissions and full-text search; CCDC acts as official depository for 51 major journals and provides quality metrics and analysis tools.

## Chemical Identifiers & Structure Notation

**SMILES (Simplified Molecular-Input Line-Entry System)**
- **Authority**: Originally Daylight Chemical Information Systems; now widely adopted
- **URL**: https://chem.libretexts.org/Courses/Fordham_University/Chem1102:_Drug_Discovery_-_From_the_Laboratory_to_the_Clinic/05:_Organic_Molecules/5.08:_Line_Notation_(SMILES_and_InChI)
- **Format**: ASCII line notation
- **Access/License**: Free standard
- **Description**: Line notation for chemical structures using short ASCII strings; widely supported by cheminformatics software; human-readable but not canonical (multiple valid SMILES for same structure).

**InChI/InChIKey (IUPAC International Chemical Identifier)**
- **Authority**: International Union of Pure and Applied Chemistry
- **URL**: https://www.inchi-trust.org/downloads/
- **Format**: Character-string identifier + software tools
- **Access/License**: Free, open standard since 2005
- **Description**: Unique, canonical molecular identifier encoding connectivity, stereochemistry, isotopic composition, and charge; machine-readable and globally unambiguous; InChIKey provides fixed-length hash for database searching.

**Chemical Identifiers Overview**
- **Authority**: Chemistry LibreTexts (educational)
- **URL**: https://chem.libretexts.org/Courses/University_of_Arkansas_Little_Rock/ChemInformatics_(2015):_Chem_4399_5399/Text/5_Chemical_Identifiers
- **Format**: Web tutorial
- **Access/License**: Free educational content
- **Description**: Comparative guide to InChI, SMILES, molecular weight, chemical name, and CAS numbers for chemical structure representation and identification.

## Chemical Safety Reporting Guidelines

**ACS Guidelines for Chemical Laboratory Safety in Academic Institutions**
- **Authority**: American Chemical Society Committee on Chemical Safety
- **URL**: https://www.acs.org/content/dam/acsorg/about/governance/committees/chemicalsafety/publications/acs-safety-guidelines-academic.pdf
- **Format**: PDF guideline document
- **Access/License**: Free download
- **Description**: Comprehensive framework covering hazard identification, risk assessment, minimization strategies (R.A.M.P. protocol), emergency preparedness, and institutional safety culture for academic research settings.

**ACS Safety Guides & Tip Sheets Collection**
- **Authority**: American Chemical Society
- **URL**: https://www.acs.org/about/governance/committees/chemical-safety/publications-resources/guides-and-tip-sheets.html
- **Format**: Web resource library (PDFs)
- **Access/License**: Free downloads
- **Description**: Topical safety guides on spill response, hazard identification, laboratory design, and chemical incident reporting; supporting materials for laboratory safety programs and compliance.

**Identifying and Evaluating Hazards in Research Laboratories**
- **Authority**: ACS Committee on Chemical Safety
- **URL**: https://www.acs.org/content/dam/acsorg/about/governance/committees/training/acsapproved/degreeprogram/laboratory-safety.pdf
- **Format**: PDF guide
- **Access/License**: Free download
- **Description**: Task-based hazard identification methodology for research settings; helps assess chemical, physical, biological, and ergonomic risks; integrates into institutional safety culture and training programs.

## Open Data Standards & Repositories

**FAIR Data Principles in Chemistry**
- **Authority**: NFDI4Chem / Multiple publishers
- **URL**: https://knowledgebase.nfdi4chem.de/knowledge_base/docs/fair/
- **Format**: Interactive knowledge base
- **Access/License**: Free educational resource
- **Description**: Implementation guide for Findable, Accessible, Interoperable, Reusable (FAIR) data principles in chemistry research; covers metadata standards, open formats (AnIML, JCAMP-DX, mzML), and best practices for long-term data stewardship.

**PubChem Substance & Compound Databases**
- **Authority**: National Institutes of Health / National Center for Biotechnology Information
- **URL**: https://pubchem.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/submit
- **Format**: Web database + submission portal
- **Access/License**: Free access and submission
- **Description**: Public chemical structure repository accepting submissions from journals, vendors, screening centers, and researchers; standardizes structures via automated processes; serves as de-facto archive for chemical metadata and bioassay data.

**PubChem Chemical Structure Standardization**
- **Authority**: NIH / Journal of Cheminformatics
- **URL**: https://jcheminf.biomedcentral.com/articles/10.1186/s13321-018-0293-8
- **Format**: Peer-reviewed article (open access)
- **Access/License**: Free full-text
- **Description**: Technical documentation of PubChem's automated structure standardization workflow; explains tautomeric handling, salt/salt-form normalization, and aromaticity perception; addresses common challenges in cheminformatics data processing.

**IUPAC FAIR Chemistry Cookbook**
- **Authority**: International Union of Pure and Applied Chemistry
- **URL**: https://iupac.github.io/WFChemCookbook/datasources/pubchem.html
- **Format**: Interactive tutorial + documentation
- **Access/License**: Free online
- **Description**: Practical recipes for implementing FAIR data practices in chemistry workflows; includes guidance on PubChem submissions, metadata requirements, and integration with research data management systems.

**FAIR and Open Data in Science: Chemistry Perspective**
- **Authority**: De Gruyter / IUPAC
- **URL**: https://www.degruyterbrill.com/document/doi/10.1515/ci-2021-0304/html
- **Format**: Perspective article
- **Access/License**: Free full-text (open access)
- **Description**: Strategic overview of FAIR and open data adoption in chemistry; discusses implementation challenges, benefits for research reproducibility, and role of community standards in enabling data reuse.

**NMReDATA Initiative & nmrXiv Platform**
- **Authority**: Community-led effort
- **URL**: https://nmredatainitiative.github.io/
- **Format**: GitHub pages + community repository
- **Access/License**: Free, open-source initiative
- **Description**: Community-driven effort to establish NMReDATA as the standard for NMR data reporting in publications; includes tools, examples, and integration guides for scientific software and manuscript preparation workflows.
