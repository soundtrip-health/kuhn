# Story 006: Recommendation & Decision

**Status:** draft
**Epic:** [001 — Editor Foundation Research](../index.md)
**Estimate:** M

## Goal

Synthesize findings from all prior stories into a recommendation document. Present trade-offs clearly enough for a final decision.

## Acceptance Criteria

- [ ] Recommendation document written covering:
  - Summary of each candidate's strengths and weaknesses
  - License implications and risk assessment
  - Extensibility assessment based on the spike
  - Estimated effort to reach MVP for each approach
  - Clear recommendation with reasoning
- [ ] Decision made and recorded
- [ ] Architecture doc (`docs/architecture.md`) updated to reflect the chosen foundation
- [ ] Follow-on epics drafted based on the decision

## Notes

- The recommendation should present options, not just one answer — the final call involves product and business considerations beyond pure engineering
- Depends on all prior stories being complete (or substantially so)
- The decision will unblock all subsequent epics (tech stack, initial scaffold, etc.)
