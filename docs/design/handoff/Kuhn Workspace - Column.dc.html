Psilocybin-Assisted Therapy for Treatment-Resistant Depression

A Phase 2, randomized, double-blind, placebo-controlled trial

1  Background and Rationale

Treatment-resistant depression affects roughly a third of patients with major depressive disorder and is associated with substantial morbidity and cost. Recent randomized trials of psilocybin therapy have reported rapid and durable reductions in depressive symptomsGoodwin 2022, motivating a confirmatory Phase 2 evaluation in this population.

Writer is drafting The present study builds on this evidence by evaluating a single 25 mg dose administered with structured psychological support
