Clinical Trial Protocol · v0.3

Psilocybin-Assisted Therapy for Treatment-Resistant Depression

A Phase 2, randomized, double-blind, placebo-controlled trial

1  Background and Rationale

Treatment-resistant depression — major depressive disorder that has not responded to at least two adequate antidepressant trials — affects roughly a third of patients and accounts for a disproportionate share of the disorder's morbidity and costRush 2006.

Recent randomized trials of psilocybin therapy report rapid, durable reductions in depressive symptomsGoodwin 2022, including in treatment-resistant populationsCarhart-Harris 2021. This protocol specifies a confirmatory Phase 2 evaluation.

2  Objectives and Endpoints

This trial is organized around one primary efficacy objective and a set of key secondary objectives covering durability, function, and safety.

3  Trial Design

A multi-site, randomized, double-blind, placebo-controlled, parallel-group design. Eligible participants are randomized 1:1 to a single 25 mg dose of psilocybin or placebo, each administered with standardized psychological support across preparation, dosing, and integration visits.
